7-day home check

This is the best way to know your true blood pressure. For 7 days, take 2 readings every morning and 2 every evening, 1 minute apart. Day 1 is practice and isn't counted. Show the average to your health worker.

Your activity this week

Aim for at least 150 minutes of brisk activity a week (for example, 30 minutes on 5 days). A study of 270 trials found isometric exercise, such as wall sits, lowered blood pressure the most, by about 8/4 mmHg on average.

Calm breathing

Breathe in as the circle grows and out as it shrinks. Helpful when you feel stressed.

Wall sit or hand squeeze

Hold for 2 minutes, rest for 2 minutes, 4 times. Do this 3 times a week. Keep breathing normally and never hold your breath. Don't do it if your reading is 180/110 or higher. If you have heart problems, ask your health worker first.

Easy ways to lower stress and move more

  • Walk for 5 minutes after every meal or long sitting.
  • Use the stairs. Walk while you talk on the phone.
  • Try 5 minutes of calm breathing or quiet prayer before bed.
  • Sleep 7 to 9 hours. Keep the same bedtime.
  • Spend time with family and friends.
  • Drink less alcohol, and no tea, coffee or cigarettes for 30 minutes before a reading.

Key facts

High blood pressure is usually silentMost people feel nothing at all. The only way to know is to measure it.
Keep taking your medicineBlood pressure medicine works only while you take it. Feeling well means it's working.
Less saltWHO advises less than 5 g of salt a day, about one level teaspoon in total. Seasoning cubes, stock powder, instant noodles and salted fish add a lot.
Herbs don't replace medicineHerbal mixtures can interact with your tablets. Tell your health worker about anything you take.
Eat more plantsVegetables (such as ugu, spinach and bitter leaf), fruit, beans and whole grains help lower blood pressure.
Move every dayBrisk walking, farm work, dancing and wall sits all lower blood pressure over time.

How to take a good reading

  1. No tea, coffee, smoking or exercise for 30 minutes before. Use the toilet first.
  2. Sit on a chair with your back supported and both feet flat. Don't cross your legs.
  3. Rest quietly for 5 minutes. Don't talk during the reading.
  4. Put the cuff on your bare upper arm, at the level of your heart, with your arm resting on a table.
  1. Use the right cuff size. A cuff that's too small gives readings that are too high.
  2. Take 2 readings 1 minute apart. If they're very different, take a third.
  3. Use an upper-arm machine that has been tested for accuracy. Wrist machines are less reliable. Check validatebp.org or the BIHS list.
  4. Measure at about the same times each day.

How Steady reads your numbers

If the top and bottom numbers fall in different bands, the higher band counts. Home readings are usually lower than clinic readings, so most guidelines use lower home cut-offs (135/85). Steady isn't designed for pregnancy or children, which use different rules.

Use Steady without internet

  1. Open Steady once while you have internet.
  2. Android (Chrome): tap Install app at the top, or the ⋮ menu, then Add to Home screen.
  3. iPhone (Safari): tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.
  4. Open Steady from the icon on your home screen. It works without data or Wi-Fi, and uses very little battery.
